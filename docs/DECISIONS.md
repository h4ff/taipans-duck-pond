# Locked Decisions

## Purpose

A reusable, data-driven interactive Duck Pond for the Taipans Cricket Club. New ducks enter weekly and previous ducks remain in the pond.

## World

- 16:9 primary format.
- Slightly elevated view.
- Pond occupies most of the scene.
- Scoreboard sits toward the left.
- Pavilion sits toward the right and faces the appropriate direction.
- Short pier enters from the front-left.
- Grassy point on the right is reserved for the Taipan.
- World should eventually resemble the actual club ground.

## Duck

- Rubber-duck-like body is preferred because the land-to-water transition is easier.
- Correct cap logo must eventually be curved and moulded to the cap surface.
- Core expressions include angry and sad.
- Ducks should float listlessly rather than constantly perform.
- Individual timing differences create personality.

## Version discipline

Puddle proves the complete loop before final artwork or extra features.
The snake, CSV import, exports, weather, and elaborate easter eggs are backlog items.
